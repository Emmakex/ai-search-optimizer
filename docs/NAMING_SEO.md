# AI Search Optimizer — Naming & SEO

Status: **Canonical naming baseline**  
Last reviewed: **10 September 2026**

## Canonical identity

```text
Commercial product: AI Search Optimizer
Descriptor: AI Search & llms.txt Optimization for WordPress
Repository: ai-search-optimizer
Plugin folder target: ai-search-optimizer
Main plugin file: ai-search-optimizer.php
Text domain: ai-search-optimizer
WordPress.org target slug: ai-search-optimizer (not yet reserved/approved)
System: Kairoseth Extensions
Host: WordPress / WooCommerce
```

Use **AI Search Optimizer** as the primary customer-facing product name. Kairoseth is the ecosystem/brand, not part of the SEO slug.

## Technical compatibility identity

The standalone product is descended from **Kairoseth AI Web Readiness Connector 0.3.2**. During the 0.4.0 extraction, these implementation identifiers remain intentionally unchanged:

```text
kairoseth-ai-web-readiness/v1
kairoseth_ai_web_readiness_deploy
kairoseth_ai_web_deployer
kairoseth_ai_web_readiness_deployment
kairoseth_ai_web_readiness_setup_version
kairoseth_ai_web_readiness_llms
```

Do not rename them merely for cosmetics. A future change requires a versioned migration/backward-compatibility plan.

## Messaging

Preferred:

> Prepare, validate and publish WordPress content for the AI-search web.

> Generate and manage a source-grounded `llms.txt` for WordPress with optional deeper optimization through Kairoseth.

Avoid:

- “rank #1 in ChatGPT”;
- “guaranteed AI citations”;
- “forces AI crawlers to index your site”;
- “gets your content into model training”;
- claims that `llms.txt`, GEO, AEO or LLMO guarantees any third-party result.

## WordPress.org naming boundary

The target slug is **not considered owned or available until WordPress.org actually approves/reserves it**. Repository naming does not prove directory slug availability.
